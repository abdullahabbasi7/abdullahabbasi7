## Featured projects

### [PULSE](https://github.com/abdullahabbasi7/PULSE) &nbsp;·&nbsp; AI platform for catering and event companies

*Final-year project, team of three*

A 12-module AI platform planned for catering and event companies, covering smart bookings, demand forecasting, QR inventory, route planning, face attendance, OCR finance and an AI concierge. Bookings, inventory and staff attendance are built so far, as a multi-tenant web app with PostgreSQL row-level security.

**My module: Booking and Live Event Board, with a smart inquiry reader**

- Reads a WhatsApp or email inquiry in English or Roman Urdu and fills in the booking form
- Classifies the event type with TF-IDF and logistic regression
- Extracts date, time, guest count, venue and dishes with a maximum-entropy BIO sequence tagger
- Gets **98.6% of fields correct** on 20 hand-written test messages phrased differently from the training patterns
- Trained on generated data, because no public Roman Urdu catering dataset exists

Teammates' modules: QR inventory with equipment prediction (Amna Arslan) and face check-in with liveness detection (Atiqa Jabeen).

`Python` `FastAPI` `PostgreSQL` `scikit-learn` `React` `Docker`

### [Real Estate AI](https://github.com/abdullahabbasi7/realestate-ai) &nbsp;·&nbsp; Multi-agent investment memo generator

*Personal project, prototype*

Takes a property address and passes it through four LLM agents in sequence: property analyst, market scout, valuation modeler and synthesis. The result is an investment memo. It runs on a local Ollama model or Groq's free cloud tier.

`LangGraph` `LangChain` `FastAPI` `SQLite` `Next.js` `TypeScript`
